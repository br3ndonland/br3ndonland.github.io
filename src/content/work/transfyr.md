Transfyr recruited me from Formation Bio to join their team. I'm aligned with Transfyr's goal of building "the observability layer for science." The problems Transfyr is solving are the same ones that originally motivated my transition from science research into tech (for more about this, see [here](/work/harvard#transition-into-tech) and [here](/projects/nrf1-proteomics)). It's rewarding to be solving these problems.

As a Member of Technical Staff, I work on all things software, including full stack software engineering, data engineering, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, and more.
