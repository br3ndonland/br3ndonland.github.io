## Comments

In the spirit of tech [awesome lists](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome), I've been putting together lists of "awesome" Blu-ray and UHD discs each year, starting in 2021 and 2022 when upgrading my collection to 4K UHD.

Discs make the list primarily based on my subjective evaluation of their technical merits. They are listed in order of original release date, with boxsets positioned based on the first film or episode in the set. They are not ranked or scored.

Films are sometimes released by multiple distributors, so some may be mentioned multiple times.
